# Primary sources: fbt

Read 29 Sep 2026 unless stated. Figures live in `data/rates/<year>.yaml` (fbt group) and `data/rates/<year>.d/fbt.yaml`, each with its own source URL.

## Legislation
- Fringe Benefits Tax Assessment Act 1986 (compilation in force 1 Jul 2026): ss 5B (gross-up), 7 to 10 (car benefits, statutory formula s 9, operating cost s 10), 8(2) (work-related vehicles), 8A (electric cars), 39A (car parking), 58P (minor benefits), 58X (work-related items), 135P (reportable fringe benefits), Div 9A (meal entertainment). https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2004A03280/2026-07-01/2026-07-01/text/original/epub/OEBPS/document_1/document_1.html
- Treasury Laws Amendment (Tax Reform No. 1) Act 2026 (No. 49 of 2026) Sch 4 Pt 2: s 24(1A) and substituted s 58X(2), applying to FBT years starting on or after 1 Apr 2027. https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2026A00049/asmade/2026-06-26/text/1/epub/OEBPS/document_1/document_1.html
- FBTAA s 58P text (minor benefits): https://summerland.austlii.edu.au/cgi-bin/viewdoc/au/legis/cth/consol_act/fbtaa1986312/s58p.html
