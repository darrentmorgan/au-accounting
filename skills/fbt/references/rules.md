# Calculation order and edge cases

The tools apply these rules. Figures are named by key; values come from the rates files (`fbt.*`).

1. **FBT year.** The income year argument selects the FBT year ending 31 March in that income year (`meta.fbt_year_ending`). Days in the FBT year come from the dates (365, or 366 where 29 February falls in it).
2. **Statutory formula.** Taxable value = base value x `fbt.car_statutory_rate` x days available / days in the FBT year, less employee contributions, not below nil. Contributions above the gross value do not carry to other benefits. Base value includes GST, luxury car tax and dealer delivery, less trade-in or cash paid by the employee; it excludes registration and stamp duty.
3. **Operating cost.** Costs = running costs + deemed depreciation (depreciated value at the start of the year x `fbt.car_operating_cost_depreciation_rate`) + deemed interest (same value x `fbt.benchmark_interest_rate`), apportioned for the days held; a leased car uses lease payments instead. Depreciated value = cost price reduced by the deemed rate for each earlier FBT year held. Taxable value = costs x private use share, less contributions. Without adequate logbook and odometer records the private share is the whole.
4. **Gross-up.** FBT = (type 1 taxable value x `fbt.gross_up_type1` + type 2 taxable value x `fbt.gross_up_type2`) x `fbt.rate`. Instalments paid are credited; quarterly instalments apply where the prior year FBT reached `fbt.instalment_prior_year_fbt_min`.
5. **Reportable amount.** Sum of the employee's reportable taxable values (type 1, type 2 and exempt electric car benefits). Reportable only if the sum is more than `fbt.rfba_reporting_threshold_taxable_value`; then grossed up at type 2 for every benefit. Shown to whole dollars, cents ignored.
6. **Loans.** Notional interest = daily balance x `fbt.benchmark_interest_rate` for the year; taxable value = notional less interest charged, not below nil; then reduced for the income-producing share of the loan (otherwise deductible rule, rate assumed set without regard to use). Normally type 2.
7. **Electric car exemption (s 8A).** All must hold: car; zero or low emissions type; later of first held and first used date on or after `fbt.ev_exemption_start_date`; current employee or associate; no luxury car tax payable (value at first retail sale not above the fuel-efficient threshold for the financial year of sale, `fbt.lct_threshold_fuel_efficient_by_year`). Plug-in hybrids from `fbt.phev_cutoff_date` need exempt use before that date and a financially binding commitment continuing unchanged. Exempt benefits are still reportable. The `fbt.ev_proposal_*` figures describe an exposure draft and are never applied.
8. **Work vehicles (s 8(2)).** Eligible vehicle type, and private use limited to home to work, incidental work travel and minor, infrequent and irregular use. PCG 2018/3 limits are `fbt.pcg_2018_3_*`: not more than the private kilometre cap, no return journey above the trip cap, diversion not above the diversion cap, plus a written policy and an employee assurance.
9. **Car parking (s 39A).** Threshold `fbt.car_parking_threshold`; the commercial station's lowest representative all-day fee must be above it on the first day of the FBT year and on the day. Small business exemption uses `fbt.small_business_turnover_threshold` or `fbt.small_business_gross_income_threshold` for the prior income year.
10. **Meal entertainment.** 50/50 share is `fbt.meal_entertainment_split_rate` of total spend; the register share is register employee-and-associate value over register total value.
11. **Minor benefits.** Notional taxable value must be less than `fbt.minor_benefit_threshold`, per benefit; then the five s 58P(1)(f) factors.
12. **Due dates.** `fbt.return_due_date` for self-lodged returns and payment; `fbt.return_due_date_agent_electronic` where a tax agent lodges electronically and the employer was on the agent's FBT client list by the first date. A date that is a Saturday, Sunday or a public holiday for the whole of any State, the ACT or the NT moves to the first business day after (TAA 1953 s 8AAZMB; Sch 1 s 388-52); the tool returns the reason in `business_day_roll`.

Boundary conventions: reporting threshold, parking threshold and small business tests are strict ("more than", "greater than", "less than"); the minor benefit test is "less than"; the PCG kilometre limits are "not more than".
