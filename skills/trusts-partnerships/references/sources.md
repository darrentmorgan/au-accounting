# Primary sources: trusts-partnerships

Read 29 Sep 2026 unless stated. Figures live in `data/rates/<year>.d/trusts.yaml` (and the base files), each with its own source URL.

## Legislation
- Income Tax Rates Act 1986 (compilation in force 1 Jul 2026): s12(9) (s99A rate), s13 (Div 6AA resident rates, phase-in cap s13(2), trustee rates s13(3)-(7), resident phase-out limit formula s13(10)), s15 (non-resident minors), s28 (s98 trustee rates), Sch 7, Sch 11, Sch 12. https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2004A03348/2026-07-01/2026-07-01/text/original/epub/OEBPS/document_1/document_1.html
- Income Tax Assessment Act 1936: Div 5 (ss90, 92 partnerships), Div 6 (ss95, 97, 98, 98A, 99, 99A, 100, 100A), Div 6D (trustee beneficiaries), Div 6E, Div 6AA (ss102AC excepted persons, 102AE excepted income, 102AG trust income), Div 7A Subdiv EA, Part IVA, Sch 2F (trust losses, family trust elections, Div 271 family trust distribution tax). https://www.legislation.gov.au/C1936A00027/latest/text
  - s102AC: https://www.austlii.edu.au/au/legis/cth/consol_act/itaa1936240/s102ac.html
  - s102AE: https://www.austlii.edu.au/au/legis/cth/consol_act/itaa1936240/s102ae.html
- Income Tax Assessment Act 1997: Subdiv 115-C (s115-215 gross-up, s115-228 specific entitlement to capital gains), Subdiv 207-B (s207-58 specific entitlement to franked distributions), s61-115 (LITO and minors' eligible income). https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2004A05138/latest/text
  - s207-58: https://www.austlii.edu.au/au/legis/cth/consol_act/itaa1997240/s207.58.html
