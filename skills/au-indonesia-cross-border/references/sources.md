- Indonesian Directorate General of Taxes regulation PER-43/PJ/2011 on the domestic residence of individuals is SOURCE-CITED only (currency not checked): https://pajak.go.id/en/node/62005. The plugin does not apply it; Indonesian domestic residency and tax questions go to AU-IDN-002.

## Open points recorded in the brief
- The day-count method for Art 5(2)(j), a fixed base for a Bali home office or desk, "permanent home available" for a let-out villa, the effect of the MLI on ITAA 1953 s 3A(4) for Art 13(4), and the treatment of trusts under the agreement are not settled by the text. The skill escalates them.
- Three MLI-derived figures (`au_indonesia.mli_splitting_up_min_activity_days`, `mli_land_rich_lookback_days`, `mli_closely_related_control_share`) are SOURCE-CITED because they were read only in the ATO synthesised text, which is not a source of law.
- ATO FITO guide 2026 Example 16 implies a Medicare threshold that differs from the ATO Medicare low-income page; the tools use the Medicare page figures in the base file. Do not copy the guide example's total for 2025-26.
