# Primary sources: residency-cross-border

Read 29 Sep 2026 unless stated. Figures live in `data/rates/<year>.d/residency.yaml`, each with its own source URL.

## Legislation
- ITAA 1936 s 6(1) (definition of resident): quoted in TR 2023/1 para 11. https://www.ato.gov.au/law/view/document?docid=TXR/TR20231/NAT/ATO/00001
- ITAA 1936 s 23AG (exemption of income earned in overseas employment; current wording including s 23AG(1AA)): https://www.austlii.edu.au/cgi-bin/viewdoc/au/legis/cth/consol_act/itaa1936240/s23ag.html
- ITAA 1997 s 770-75 (foreign income tax offset limit): https://www.ato.gov.au/law/view/document?docid=PAC%2F19970038%2F770-75
- ITAA 1997 Div 770 (foreign income tax offset), s 770-70 amount of offset. Original insertion text: https://www.ato.gov.au/law/view/pdf/acts/20070143.pdf
- ITAA 1997 Subdiv 768-R, s 768-910 (income derived by temporary resident), s 768-915 (gains and losses disregarded): https://www.austlii.edu.au/au/legis/cth/consol_act/itaa1997240/s768.915.html
- ITAA 1997 s 104-160 (CGT event I1): https://www.ato.gov.au/law/view/document?docid=PAC%2F19970038%2F104-160
- ITAA 1997 s 104-165 (individual's choice to disregard the gain or loss): https://www.austlii.edu.au/cgi-bin/viewdoc/au/legis/cth/consol_act/itaa1997240/s104.165.html
- Treasury Laws Amendment (Strengthening Accountability for Tax Adviser Misconduct and Other Measures) Act 2026 (foreign resident CGT changes from 1 Oct 2026): https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2026A00086/asmade/2026-09-15/text/original/epub/OEBPS/document_1/document_1.html
