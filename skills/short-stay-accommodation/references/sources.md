## Corrections to earlier repo text (found by the gate 9 research)
- GSTR 2012/6 Example 12 is in the current ruling (paras 82 to 85), not only in the draft.
- SERR is not limited to bookings of a set number of consecutive days; the 2025 instrument has no such concept (the superseded 2023 determination defined relevant accommodation by consecutive days).
- The platform-fee GST and ABN-declaration passage is ride-sourcing guidance, not accommodation guidance.
- TR 2026/1 replaces IT 2167; it does not replace TR 97/23.
- The short-stay dwelling is not outside the 2027-28 loss quarantine.
