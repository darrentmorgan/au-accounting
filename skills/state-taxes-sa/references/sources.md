# Primary sources: state-taxes-sa

Read 29 Sep 2026 unless stated. Figures live in `data/rates/<year>.yaml` (key `state.sa`) and `data/rates/<year>.d/state_sa.yaml`, each with its own source.

## Payroll tax
- Payroll Tax Act 2009 (SA), version 1.7.2024 (s 9 payment dates, s 11 nexus, ss 32-41 relevant contracts and employment agents, ss 67-80 grouping, s 83 annual adjustment, Sch 1 cl 4 and 5 annual liability, Sch 2 cl 5 and 6 monthly liability and deduction, Sch 2 cl 21 disregarding cents): https://www.legislation.sa.gov.au/__legislation/lz/c/a/payroll%20tax%20act%202009/current/2009.22.auth.pdf
- Schedule 2 text: http://classic.austlii.edu.au/au/legis/sa/consol_act/pta2009155/sch2.html
- How payroll tax is calculated (flat deduction, worked examples, part-year annualisation): https://www.revenuesa.sa.gov.au/payroll-tax/how-is-payroll-tax-calculated
- Rates and thresholds: https://www.revenuesa.sa.gov.au/payroll-tax/rates-and-thresholds
- Monthly returns and lodgement dates (annual reconciliation date): https://www.revenuesa.sa.gov.au/payrolltax/returns-and-annual-reconciliation/monthly-returns
- 2022-23 Guide to Legislation, payroll tax (monthly and group worked examples): https://www.revenuesa.sa.gov.au/payroll-tax/forms-and-publications/guide-to-legislation-payroll-tax/2022-23-guide-to-legislation-payroll-tax

## Land tax
- Land Tax Act 1936 (SA), version 1.7.2023 as served: https://www.legislation.sa.gov.au/__legislation/lz/c/a/land%20tax%20act%201936/current/1936.2318.auth.pdf
- Section 5 exemptions (AustLII): https://classic.austlii.edu.au/au/legis/sa/consol_act/lta193690/s5.html
- Rates and thresholds, 2026-27 general and trust scales (page updated 12 Jun 2026): https://www.revenuesa.sa.gov.au/land-tax/rates-and-thresholds
- Land held on trust: https://www.revenuesa.sa.gov.au/land-tax/trusts
- Principal place of residence exemption and partial exemption table: https://www.revenuesa.sa.gov.au/landtax/exemption-waiver-relief/residential-home
- Primary production exemption: https://www.revenuesa.sa.gov.au/landtax/exemption-waiver-relief/primary-production
- 2025-26 Guide to Legislation, land tax: https://www.revenuesa.sa.gov.au/resources/publications/guides-to-legislation/guide-to-legislation-land-tax2/2025-26-guide-to-legislation-land-tax

## Stamp duty
- Stamp Duties Act 1923 (SA): s 71DB (off-the-plan, expired for contracts after 30 Jun 2017) http://www5.austlii.edu.au/au/legis/sa/consol_act/sda1923157/s71db.html ; s 71DD (first home buyer relief) http://classic.austlii.edu.au/au/legis/sa/consol_act/sda1923157/s71dd.html
- Rate of stamp duty (conveyance scale): https://www.revenuesa.sa.gov.au/stamp-duty-land/rate-of-stamp-duty
- Foreign ownership surcharge: https://www.revenuesa.sa.gov.au/stamp-duty-land/FOS
- Real property (land), qualifying land: https://www.revenuesa.sa.gov.au/stamp-duty-land/real-property-land
- First home buyer relief available (contract periods): https://www.revenuesa.sa.gov.au/stampduty/first-home-buyer-relief/relief-available
- Seniors downsizing relief (not verified; RevenueSA page could not be fetched): https://www.revenuesa.sa.gov.au/stamp-duty-land/seniors-downsizing-stamp-duty-relief
- Public Holidays Act 2023 (SA) s 8 (payment or action on a public holiday, bank holiday or Saturday moves to the next day that is not one; s 8(2) is the only candidate rule for land tax due dates): https://www.legislation.sa.gov.au/lz?path=/c/a/public%20holidays%20act%202023
- Legislation Interpretation Act 2021 (SA) s 44(2) (last day not a business day): https://www.legislation.sa.gov.au/lz?path=/c/a/legislation%20interpretation%20act%202021
- RevenueSA, monthly returns and lodgement dates (includes the December return extension): https://www.revenuesa.sa.gov.au/payrolltax/returns-and-annual-reconciliation/monthly-returns
- Public holiday data and the rule analysis: data/holidays/au.yaml and docs/research/due-dates-public-holidays.md
