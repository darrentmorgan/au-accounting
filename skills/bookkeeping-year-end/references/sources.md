# Primary sources: bookkeeping-year-end

Read 29 Sep 2026 unless stated. Figures live in `data/rates/<year>.d/bookkeeping.yaml` (and `asic.large_proprietary_*` in the base files) each with its own source.

## Legislation
- ITAA 1936 s 262A (records); TAA 1953 Sch 1 Div 382, s 382-5. https://www.legislation.gov.au/C1936A00027/latest/text
- ITAA 1936 ss 82KZL-82KZMD (prepayments; s 82KZM 12-month rule; s 82KZL excluded expenditure). https://www.ato.gov.au/law/view/document?docid=SAV%2FDPE%2F00001
- ITAA 1997 s 25-35 (bad debts), s 165-120 (company bad debts), s 70-35 and s 70-45 (trading stock), s 328-285 (simplified trading stock). https://www.austlii.edu.au/au/legis/cth/consol_act/itaa1997240/s328.285.html ; https://www.austlii.edu.au/cgi-bin/viewdoc/au/legis/cth/consol_act/itaa1997240/s165.120.html
- GSTA 1999 s 9-70 (rate), s 29-40 (cash basis), Div 21 (bad debts). https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2004A00446/latest/text/1
- Corporations Act 2001 s 45A (proprietary company size), s 286 (financial records, 7 years), ss 292-294 (who must report), s 319 (lodgment). https://classic.austlii.edu.au/au/legis/cth/consol_act/ca2001172/s45a.html ; https://classic.austlii.edu.au/au/legis/cth/consol_act/ca2001172/s286.html
- Corporations Regulations 2001 reg 1.0.02B (prescribed thresholds). https://www.legislation.gov.au/F2001B00274/latest/text
