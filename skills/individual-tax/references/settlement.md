# Deterministic individual settlement

Use `individual_tax_settlement` for an income-year refund or amount owing. It recomputes tax from taxable income and the same individual-tax facts, including `upstream_limitations` unchanged from assembly. Any limitation blocks settlement, even a conditional assumption. It never trusts a supplied liability or nets separately produced offsets. Company/trust assessments and offsets beyond existing LITO/WATO and eligible refundable franking/PHI remain outside scope.

## Required reconciliation inputs

- `tax_withheld`: confirmed income-year PAYG withholding; explicit nil when absent.
- `payg_instalment_credit`: ATO-reconciled year credit, net of variation credits. TAA Sch 1 s45-30 credits instalments payable even if unpaid; unpaid instalment debts remain on the account. Never use a projected instalment amount or arbitrary account payment as this credit.
- `franking`: explicit null confirms none; otherwise supply `income_year`, `credit`, `gross_up_included` and `refundable_eligibility_confirmed`. Dividend and franking gross-up must already be included once in taxable income. Confirm resident-individual entitlement, qualified-person/holding-period and related-payment rules and integrity restrictions. A company's maximum credit does not establish shareholder entitlement. Unresolved trust distribution entitlement/allocation belongs in `unmodelled_inputs`.
- `private_health_rebate`: explicit null confirms no adjustment; otherwise supply `income_year`, `eligibility_and_allocation_confirmed`, `income_for_rebate`, `family`, `dependent_children` and all allocated `rows`. Confirm complying policy/insurer, Medicare eligibility, tax claim code and allocation between adults. Family status and dependants are confirmed PHI facts, not inferred from the policy's name. `income_for_rebate` is confirmed income for surcharge purposes, combined for a family with applicable add-backs. Unresolved spouse claims, changed family status, ineligible periods or income-test additions require review before confirming the inputs.
- Each PHI row supplies `period` (`1jul_to_31mar` or `1apr_to_30jun`), `age_band` (`under_65`, `65_69`, `70_plus`), `eligible_premium` and `rebate_received`. Use premium payment date and the oldest covered person's applicable band; split at those boundaries. Premium is this taxpayer's allocated gross rebate-eligible amount before reductions, excluding lifetime health cover loading. Rebate received is the allocated reduction/payment already received. Aggregate duplicate portions of the same statement row. Entitlement is rounded to cents per supplied row; retain this working paper convention for agent reconciliation.
- `unmodelled_inputs` is required. Empty means reconciliation confirmed no other offsets, credits or unresolved assessment inputs; otherwise name each outstanding item, including FITO, SBITO or special credits. Never clear the list to obtain a headline. Unknown fields, including nested fields, are rejected.

## Calculation and primary authority

1. Compute tax after capped non-refundable LITO/WATO, Medicare levy, MLS and HELP with the existing individual calculator. ITAA 1997 s63-10 orders non-refundable offsets before refundable offsets. LITO/WATO cannot generate refunds or reduce levy/HELP.
2. Calculate PHI entitlement with the selected year's `medicare.phi_rebate_<period>_<age_band>`. Use single or family tier edges; the PHI family increase for qualifying children after the first uses the shared income-for-surcharge figure `medicare.mls_family_per_child_after_first`. Each premium period needs its own published table. Missing tables refuse AU-GEN-003; never borrow another year.
3. Reconcile total entitlement less total rebate received. A shortfall is a refundable offset; an excess is an additional liability, never an income deduction (ITAA 1997 Subdiv 61-G; ATO PHI claiming guidance).
4. Liability is tax after non-refundable offsets plus levy, MLS, HELP and excess PHI rebate. Credits are PAYG withholding, reconciled instalments, eligible refundable franking and PHI shortfall. Franking is credited once and not capped at income tax (ITAA 1997 ss207-20, 67-25 and Div67). Refundable offsets can produce a refund when income tax is nil. Offsets do not reduce HELP repayment income or compulsory repayment; PS LA 2011/21 Attachment A explains PAYG credit priority for study loans.
5. `net_amount_owing` is liability less credits: positive means owing, negative means refund. `refund` and `amount_owing` show separate non-negative outcomes; exact balance gives both nil. Quote values as returned.

Output includes `rule_authorities` with primary ATO URLs and applied `income_year`, plus `figures_used`. See `sources.md`. This computes an income-year assessment working paper, not an ATO notice or guaranteed cash refund. Prior account debts, unpaid instalments, retained refunds, interest and penalties require separate account reconciliation.

AU-IND-005 blocks final settlement for any unmodelled input, unresolved entitlement, mismatched year, component limitation or draft figure. Existing individual-tax refusals still apply. Preserve available component papers, quote the refusal verbatim and report **final settlement not computed**. Never replace a refusal with a withholding-only estimate.
