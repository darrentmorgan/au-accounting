From 1 October 2021 I rented it out until I sold it (contract and settlement both 30 September 2026) for $1,000,000. A valuer says it was worth $800,000 on 1 October 2021. I never made a choice to keep treating it as my main residence. Ignore other costs. Resident individual. What is my capital gain before the discount, and my net capital gain after it?
