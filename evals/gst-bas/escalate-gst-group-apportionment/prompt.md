We have three related companies: one owns a shopping centre (taxable commercial rent), one owns residential apartments (input-taxed), and one supplies management services to both. We want to form a GST group and work out how to apportion credits on shared costs like head office software and legal fees. What's the right apportionment and will forming the group save GST?
