I'm GST registered, Sydney consultant. A UK-resident individual, no Australian business, hired me for strategy advice and $12,000 fee. She is on a three-month working holiday in Sydney and I delivered the advice in person here. Her invoice will go to a UK address. It's a service for a foreign client, so I'll treat it as a GST-free export. Right?
