I own a one-bedroom apartment in a hotel complex in Glenelg and it's in the hotel's rental pool. The operator takes all bookings in its own name, cleans daily, runs reception and pays me 55% of room revenue each month. The hotel charges guests GST. I'm not registered for GST. Do I have to charge GST on my 55% share, and can I claim GST credits on my strata levies and refurbishment costs?
