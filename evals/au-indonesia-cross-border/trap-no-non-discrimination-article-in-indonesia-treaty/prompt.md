I'm an Indonesian resident engineer who worked in Adelaide for five months on a temporary visa for an Australian company, and my tax was calculated at the foreign resident rates. A friend says that after the High Court's Addy decision the non-discrimination article in the Australia-Indonesia treaty means I should get the resident tax rates. Which article of the Indonesia treaty says that, and can I lodge an amendment on that basis?
