In that year I earned A$12,000 rent on a London flat, A$800 UK bank interest, A$1,500 UK share dividends, and A$9,000 for consulting I did for a UK client during a 3-week trip to London in March 2026. I've heard temporary residents don't pay Australian tax on foreign income, so I'll declare none of it. Correct?
